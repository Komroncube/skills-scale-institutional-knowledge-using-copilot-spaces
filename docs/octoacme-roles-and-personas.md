# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Engineer

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Engineers own quality assurance, test strategy, and acceptance validation. They partner with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans aligned with Product Manager-defined acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated tests with Developers during implementation and PR validation
- Identify, document, and triage defects with Developers and Project Managers
- Validate bug fixes and release readiness before milestone sign-off
- Ensure regression coverage and track quality metrics across iterations

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features with minimal defects in production
- Reduce cycle time for quality validation
- Improve test automation coverage and reliability

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups with Developers and Project Managers
- PR reviews and defect triage discussions with Developers
- Quality metrics and release-readiness updates with Product Managers and Project Managers

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects provide technical direction, design guidance, and architectural consistency. They mentor Developers and partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to identify technical risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and propose improvements before implementation
- Identify scalability, reliability, and performance risks and align mitigation plans with Project Managers
- Mentor Developers on architecture, code quality, and engineering practices
- Guide technology decisions by balancing Product Manager priorities with long-term maintainability

### Goals
- Maintain system health and scalability
- Reduce technical debt
- Enable team capability growth

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical spike discussions with Developers
- Architecture decision record (ADR) reviews with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Mentorship sessions and technical planning support across the delivery team

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors provide business context, approvals, and resource support. They act as project champions and liaisons to executive leadership while partnering with Product Managers and Project Managers on priorities and outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project scope, timeline, and major trade-offs proposed by Product Managers and Project Managers
- Provide business requirements, constraints, and strategic context to Product Managers and Developers
- Remove organizational blockers escalated by Project Managers
- Communicate project value, progress, and decisions to leadership

### Goals
- Ensure project delivery produces measurable business value
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and decision clarity
- Secure resources and executive support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates led with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Milestone reviews and decision gates for scope, release, or investment changes
- Escalation resolution touchpoints for cross-team blockers

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into actionable requirements. They collaborate with Product Managers on discovery, Developers on implementation clarity, and Project Managers on scope and dependency tracking.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements, workflows, and acceptance details
- Refine user stories with Product Managers to keep backlog items implementation-ready
- Clarify edge cases and process impacts with Developers during design and build
- Support Project Managers with requirement traceability and change impact analysis

### Goals
- Improve requirement quality and reduce ambiguity
- Minimize rework caused by misunderstood business rules
- Strengthen alignment between business outcomes and delivered features

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and requirement workshops with Product Managers and Developers
- Scope and dependency reviews with Project Managers
- Stakeholder interviews and validation sessions for requirement sign-off

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches facilitate team effectiveness and continuous improvement. They support Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers by improving delivery flow, team rituals, and process discipline.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Coach teams on agile practices, estimation, and incremental delivery
- Surface impediments and partner with Project Managers to remove blockers
- Help Product Managers and Developers improve backlog quality and sprint predictability

### Goals
- Increase team throughput and delivery predictability
- Improve collaboration and process health
- Foster continuous improvement and sustainable pace

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremony facilitation
- Retrospective action planning with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers
- Delivery health and flow metrics discussions with team leads

---

## Documentation/Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Documentation/Technical Writers produce and maintain clear product and process documentation. They work with Developers for technical accuracy, Product Managers for user intent, and Project Managers for release-ready communication.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user guides, release notes, and process documents
- Collaborate with Developers to document technical behavior and limitations
- Align content with Product Manager messaging, terminology, and user outcomes
- Coordinate publication timing and documentation completeness with Project Managers

### Goals
- Ensure documentation is accurate, discoverable, and up to date
- Reduce support friction through clear guidance
- Improve onboarding efficiency for users and internal teams

### Typical Communication
- PR and feature review touchpoints with Developers for technical validation
- Content planning and messaging alignment with Product Managers
- Release documentation checklists and milestone readiness reviews with Project Managers
