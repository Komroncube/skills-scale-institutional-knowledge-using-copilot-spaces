# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder is the central source for how OctoAcme plans, delivers, and improves projects. Our approach is customer-first, iterative, and grounded in clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety so teams can deliver value while learning continuously.

## Project Management Lifecycle

1. **Initiation**: Confirm the problem, define measurable outcomes, align stakeholders, and decide go/no-go for planning.
2. **Planning**: Break work into shippable increments, prioritize and estimate backlog items, define Definition of Done, and map milestones/dependencies.
3. **Execution**: Build, test, review, and track delivery through regular team cadence, PR workflow, and risk monitoring.
4. **Release**: Validate readiness, deploy safely, verify production health, and communicate updates to stakeholders.
5. **Close & Retrospective**: Capture what worked, identify improvements, and convert learnings into owned follow-up actions.

## Core Roles

- **Project Manager**: Coordinates delivery, timelines, risks, dependencies, and stakeholder communication.
- **Product Manager**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes roadmap/backlog, and measures success against customer and business goals.
- **Developers**: Design, implement, test, and maintain solutions that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.
- **QA/Testing**: Validates quality, confirms acceptance criteria, and supports release confidence through testing practices.
- **Stakeholders**: Provide business context, feedback, decisions, and approvals at key project milestones.

## Getting Started

- Start with the [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md) for principles, lifecycle, and cadence.
- If launching new work, use the [Project Initiation Guide](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) to produce the one-pager, stakeholder list, and initial risks.
- For delivery planning and day-to-day execution, use the planning, execution, and risk/communication guides together as your operating playbook.
- Key artifacts live across these docs: project one-pager/charter, prioritized backlog, Definition of Done, release plan, risk register, and retrospective action items.
